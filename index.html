About Kenny

My name is Bawaya Kenneth, though I am widely known as Kenny. I am an aspiring software engineer and an emerging technology professional, building my career through practical work in web development, software support and the continued study of digital technologies.

I was born on 6 June 2005 to my mother, Nakafu Immaculate, and my father, Balikoowa Patrick. I was born and raised in Nangabo Village, within Kasangati Town Council, Wakiso District, Uganda. Nangabo is more than a place on my biography; it is the starting point of my story and an important part of the identity I carry as I pursue my ambitions.

My relationship with technology began with curiosity. In 2015, I took my first steps with computers, beginning a journey that would gradually develop into a deeper interest in how technology works and what it makes possible. In 2023, I began exploring software and the web more deliberately, turning that early curiosity towards learning, experimentation and practical creation.

Today, I am building a broad and increasingly focused foundation in technology. My work and learning include creating and maintaining websites, working with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, installing and configuring software, troubleshooting computer issues and providing software-related support for mobile devices. I also work with Microsoft Office applications and continue to strengthen the technical understanding, problem-solving ability and disciplined approach that meaningful software development demands.

I believe progress in technology comes from more than simply learning tools; it comes from understanding problems, thinking carefully, testing ideas and being willing to improve a solution until it serves its purpose. That mindset guides the way I approach each task, whether I am resolving a technical issue, refining a website or learning a new concept.

One expression of that commitment is Tzar Flix, my movie-streaming website project, which is currently in development. It is a practical environment in which I can apply my knowledge, confront real development challenges and gain experience in shaping a digital product from an idea into a working platform.

My professional journey is still taking shape, and I am ambitious about where it can lead. My long-term goal is to become a highly skilled software engineer, capable of designing, building and maintaining reliable software that addresses real needs. I do not regard expertise as a title to claim prematurely, but as a standard to earn through consistent study, deliberate practice, sound judgement and years of meaningful work. I am committed to that process, with the determination to keep learning, keep building and make each stage of my development count.

Professional identity

I am developing a practical foundation across the digital experience — from websites people interact with to the software and devices they depend on. I approach each task as an opportunity to learn, solve problems and improve the quality of my work.

  • Website development

    I build websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and publish them through GitHub. My focus is clear structure, readable design and pages that work on a phone as well as a desktop.

  • Website maintenance

    A website needs care after it goes live. I help keep sites current and working, from content updates to fixing things that stop working.

  • Software installation and configuration

    I install and set up software on computers so it is ready to use, including the Microsoft Office applications.

  • Computer troubleshooting

    When a computer is slow, misbehaving or will not do what it should, I work through the problem step by step to find the cause.

  • Mobile device software support

    Help with the software side of phones and other mobile devices, so they run the way their owners expect.

  • Digital solutions

    Where a practical digital tool or website can solve a problem, I like to build it. Tzar Flix, my current project, comes from that same interest.
